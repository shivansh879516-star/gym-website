REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

We stand behind the quality of our facility. We never lock members into predatory contracts or hold payments hostage.

14-Day "No Questions Asked" Cooling-Off Period: All newly enrolled members are entitled to cancel their agreement within 14 calendar days of initial sign-up for a 100% full refund of initiation fees and membership dues, minus only standard single-day pass rates ($20/day) for days the facility was physically accessed.

1. Monthly Recurring Membership Cancellation

Month-to-month memberships have no long-term contract lock-in.

  • Notice Period: Cancellation requires written notification submitted at least 14 calendar days before your next scheduled billing date.
  • Method: Submit via our online form, email shivansh983965@gmail.com, or message directly on WhatsApp at +91 9555514847.
  • Facility Access: You retain full access to the facility through the final paid day of your billing cycle. No further automatic charges will occur.

2. Annual Paid-in-Advance Memberships

Annual memberships offer a discounted 15% rate. If life circumstances require early termination after the initial 14-day window:

  • Relocation Exemption: If you move more than 25 miles from the facility, provide proof of new residence for an immediate pro-rated refund of unused complete months.
  • Medical Inability: If a physician provides written certification of physical inability to exercise for 90+ days, unused months are refunded pro-rata with zero administrative penalties.
  • Voluntary Early Cancellation: Past months will be retroactively calculated at the standard monthly non-discounted rate, and any remaining credit will be refunded to your original payment method.

3. Personal Training & Private Coaching Sessions

One-on-one personal coaching requires trainer scheduling reservations:

  • 24-Hour Notice: Individual coaching sessions can be rescheduled or cancelled without penalty with at least 24 hours notice.
  • Late Cancellations / No-Shows: Cancellations made under 24 hours are charged at the session rate to compensate the coach.
  • Unused Packages: Unused personal training packages remain valid for 12 months from purchase date. Unused sessions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.

4. Retail & Nutritional Merchandise

Apparel, lifting straps, and unopened supplements can be returned within 30 days of purchase with original receipt for a full refund or exchange. Opened nutritional products cannot be returned due to health safety regulations.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Stripe debit/credit or bank ACH) within 3 to 5 business days of approval. Titan Forge issues a written refund receipt and transaction confirmation ID via email.

Please review these binding terms governing facility access, equipment usage, billing schedules, and health declarations.

1. Agreement to Terms

By accessing the facilities of Titan Forge Fitness LLC ("Club", "we", "us"), registering for a trial pass, or purchasing an ongoing membership, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and all associated Club policies.

2. Physical Health Screening & Assumption of Risk

Voluntary Exercise & Medical Clearance: Strenuous resistance training, powerlifting, sprinting, and temperature exposure (sauna and cold plunge) carry inherent physiological risks, including muscle strains, ligament tears, cardiovascular events, and accidental impact.

You hereby certify that:

  • You are in good physical health and have answered the Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q) truthfully.
  • You have been advised to obtain medical clearance from a licensed physician prior to beginning any high-intensity regimen.
  • You voluntarily assume all known and unknown risks associated with the use of free weights, barbells, motorized turf equipment, and contrast therapy suites.

3. Facility Rules & Code of Conduct

To preserve a safe, respectful training culture for all athletes, members must adhere to:

Weight Re-Racking

Plates and dumbbells must be returned to their designated trees and racks immediately after sets.

Hygiene & Sanitization

Benches, bars, and turf sleds must be wiped down with hospital-grade disinfectant towels after use.

Photography & Privacy

Filming without consent of background members is strictly forbidden. Zero cameras permitted in locker rooms.

Respect & Harassment Policy

Titan Forge enforces a zero-tolerance policy against intimidation, discrimination, or abusive conduct.

4. Membership Billing, Renewal & Fees

Memberships are billed automatically on the 1st of each billing cycle to the authorized card on file.

  • No Lock-in Penalty: Month-to-month memberships may be cancelled at any time by providing 14 calendar days written notice prior to the upcoming billing cycle.
  • Freeze Privileges: Annual members may freeze their account for up to 60 calendar days per year for verified medical reasons or travel.
  • Late Fees: Unpaid invoices incur a $10 grace administrative charge after 7 calendar days of delinquency.

COOKIE & TRACKING POLICY

Learn about the cookies and local storage tokens we utilize to power site security, remember your settings, and evaluate page performance.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work properly, ensure navigational security, and provide reporting insights to site administrators.

We also use browser LocalStorage to record your consent choices (`titan_forge_cookie_consent_v1`) so we do not repeatedly interrupt your browsing sessions with cookie popups.
